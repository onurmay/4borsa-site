01 / THE PROJECT

Before an answer,
trust the question.

Quantitative research is only as credible as the data and decisions underneath it.

4BORSA is an independent, early-stage quantitative research and financial data analytics project focused on Borsa Istanbul (BIST). Its starting point is the research foundation: preserving what was known, when it was known, and how an analysis was produced.

Historical data can be revised. A fact visible today may not have been available at an earlier decision point. Point-in-time handling helps prevent that future information from silently entering a historical study.

Reproducible workflows connect inputs, transformations, and experiments. The aim is to make an analytical result inspectable and repeatable, before treating it as evidence.
